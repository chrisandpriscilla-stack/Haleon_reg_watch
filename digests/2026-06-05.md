## LEAD — GHG Protocol, Scope 2 Guidance revision

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — consultation closed 31 January 2026; revised draft due later in 2026.
- **What changed:** GHGP ran a major public consultation (Oct 2025–31 Jan 2026; 1,000+ submissions across 39 countries) on overhauling the Scope 2 Guidance. Proposals include hourly (time-matched) accounting, a deliverability/geographic-matching requirement for market-based instruments, a tighter emission-factor hierarchy, and — critically — a legacy clause for existing contracts. Revised draft later in 2026, final methodology 2027, phased implementation from 2028.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly bears on the Valle 4 Spain vPPA (300 GWh/yr, 2027–2036), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV instrument (GoO retirement) — a deliverability test and hourly matching could change whether these volumes remain claimable under the market-based method; the legacy clause for existing contracts is the key protection to track.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/release-ghg-protocol-opens-public-consultations-scope-2-and-electricity-sector-consequential

## GHG Protocol — Scope 3 Standard revision

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Phase 1 Progress Update, 31 March 2026.
- **What changed:** First substantive output of the Scope 3 revision. Proposes ≥95% coverage of required Scope 3 emissions (≤5% exclusions), mandatory disaggregation by data tier (primary/secondary/spend-based) and a new Category 16 for other value-chain activities. Full public-consultation draft expected mid-2026; final standard targeted late 2027.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Bears on the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target — a 95% coverage rule and tiered data disclosure would raise the data-quality bar for supplier-emissions reporting, including electricity covered by the REV GoO instrument.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/S3-Phase1ProgressUpdate-20260331.pdf

## CBAM — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism
