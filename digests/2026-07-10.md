# Reg watch — 10 July 2026

Second run. Compared against the 5 June baseline. Three material developments this week: the SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 has now been published (baseline had it as pending), the EU ETS post-2030 revision proposal is imminent, and CBAM's downstream extension has firmed up from proposal to Council agreement.

## LEAD — SBTi, Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 [PUBLISHED]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — final V2.0 published June 2026 (11 June); implementation resources released for feedback, deadline 31 July 2026.
- **What changed:** The final V2.0 is out — the item the 5 June digest was watching for. The headline shift for renewables procurement: the location-based method is now **mandatory** for Scope 2, and market-based instruments (physical and virtual PPAs, RECs/EACs, supplier contracts) are **no longer bundled into the Scope 2 target** — they sit separately under a new implementation hierarchy and must be tracked and reported apart from the location-based inventory. A 15-year age limit applies to the underlying generation assets. Supporting implementation resources (Scope 2/3, OER, removals, assurance) are out for stakeholder feedback until 31 July 2026, with finals in Q4 2026. Targets may be submitted under V1.3.1 or V2.0 from Q1 2027; V2.0 becomes mandatory for all new submissions from 31 January 2028.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly bears on the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target and on how the Valle 4 vPPA (300 GWh/yr), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO instrument count toward net-zero. Both the Valle 4 and Texas deals remain eligible market instruments (vPPAs explicitly listed), but decoupling them from the Scope 2 target and the new "clean the location-based picture first" hierarchy change how the volumes are credited and reported. The 15-year asset-age limit is comfortably met by both 2027-onward contracts. The 31 July feedback window is a live opportunity to test our procurement assumptions before the resources are finalised.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/corporate-net-zero-standard-v2

## EU ETS — post-2030 revision proposal imminent

- **Source/date:** European Commission — formal ETS revision proposal expected 17 July 2026.
- **What changed:** The Commission is due to table its post-2030 ETS revision on 17 July 2026, covering free-allocation rules, integration of permanent carbon removals, recognition of post-2040 residual emissions, the market stability reserve and simplification — reportedly adding roughly €4bn of allowances to industry. ETS2 is excluded (separate 2028 review). This supersedes the "revision targeted Q1 2027" line in the 5 June baseline: the proposal is now weeks away, not quarters.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments. Relevant only as a forward carbon-cost signal across the EU value chain and Spanish operations feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://www.opis.com/blog/eu-ets-revision-2026-what-the-july-proposal-will-look-like/

## CBAM — downstream extension reaches Council agreement

- **Source/date:** European Commission — Council agreement to strengthen CBAM, 12 June 2026.
- **What changed:** The downstream-scope extension flagged as a proposal in the 5 June baseline has firmed up: the Council has agreed to extend CBAM to ~180 additional steel- and aluminium-intensive downstream products (fabricated metals, machinery, appliances, vehicle components) from 1 January 2028, alongside anti-circumvention measures including closing the pre-consumer scrap "loophole" and tighter traceability reporting.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the electricity instruments. Watch only for input-cost effects on packaging, steel and aluminium supply chains feeding Scope 3 from 2028.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-welcomes-council-agreement-strengthening-cbam-2026-06-12_en
