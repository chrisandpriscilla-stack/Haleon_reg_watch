## SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 [PRIORITY WATCH — PUBLISHED]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — CNZS V2.0 published 11 June 2026; implementation resources now being rolled out (stakeholder feedback window open to 31 July 2026).
- **What changed:** The final V2.0 standard is out, closing the watch the baseline flagged. Target-setting and validation under V2.0 open from 1 February 2027; V1.3.1 remains the route for any target set during 2026. Priority implementation resources (Scope 2, Scope 3, removals, Ongoing Emissions Responsibility, assurance, transition planning) are being published now for feedback by 31 July 2026, with final resources due Q4 2026. Critically for procurement: Scope 2 now requires **two** targets — a location-based target **and** a separate procurement-based (market-based or zero-carbon-electricity) target — and market instruments (physical/virtual PPAs, supplier contracts, unbundled EACs) are no longer folded into the Scope 2 target but sit under a new implementation hierarchy.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly frames the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target and reshapes how the Valle 4 vPPA (300 GWh/yr, 2027–2036), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO instrument are counted. The split location/procurement structure and the market-instrument hierarchy determine whether these volumes count toward the net-zero pathway and against which target — the 31 July feedback window is the last low-cost point to influence the implementation detail.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/blog/the-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-is-here-what-comes-next

## EU ETS — Post-2030 review proposal tabled

- **Source/date:** European Commission — legislative proposal published 17 July 2026.
- **What changed:** The Commission tabled its long-trailed ETS overhaul, setting the framework for Phase 5 (2031–2040) and aligning the cap with the EU's 90%-by-2040 target. The linear reduction factor rises to 4.4% in 2028, eases to 3.7% for 2031–2035, then 1.7% from 2036, pushing allowance exhaustion out to c.2046–2048. From 2031, 80% of free allowances become conditional on published decarbonisation investment plans and 20% on verified emissions cuts. Scope expands to more maritime (ships from 400 GT) and to extra-EU flights up to 5,000 km, with Market Stability Reserve reforms and new provisions for carbon removals and international credits.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments. Relevant as a medium-term carbon-cost signal across the EU value chain and Spanish operations, feeding Scope 3 input costs; the conditional free-allocation regime and removals provisions are worth tracking for supplier cost pass-through.
- **Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1044

## GHG Protocol — Scope 2 Guidance revision (LEAD watch)

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — ISB approved market- and location-based revisions to progress to the next public consultation (mid-2026; exact blog date not retrievable — the org page returned 403).
- **What changed:** The Scope 2 revision has cleared its Independent Standards Board gate, advancing the market- and location-based changes toward the second public consultation flagged in the baseline. Confirmed elements of the market-based proposal are an **hourly matching requirement**, a **deliverability (geographic) requirement** and a new Marginal Emissions Impact metric — plus, materially, an hourly-matching exemption threshold and **transitional relief for legacy reporters**. Dual location/market reporting is retained; final standard remains slated for 2027.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** This is the item that most directly determines whether the Valle 4 vPPA, the Texas Solar PV deal and the REV GoO retirement remain claimable under the market-based method. The hourly-matching and deliverability tests could restrict claimable volumes; the transitional relief / legacy clause is the key protection for existing contracts and must be pinned down in the second consultation.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/scope-2-standard-advances-isb-approves-consultation-market-and-location-based-revisions
