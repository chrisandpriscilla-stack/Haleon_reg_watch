# Reg watch — 14 August 2026

Changes below are diffed against the 5 June 2026 baseline (the previous run). Three substantive developments have landed in the intervening period; the remaining sources show only timeline nuance or background activity.

## SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 [PRIORITY — now PUBLISHED]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — V2.0 published 11 June 2026; implementation resources rolling out through 2026.
- **What changed:** The item the baseline flagged as the key watch has now published. V2.0 consolidates the near-term criteria and the original Net-Zero Standard into a single framework and shifts emphasis from ambition-setting to implementation accountability — transition plans, an Ongoing Emissions Responsibility mechanism, and regular progress assessment are now built into the Standard. Existing sector pathways have been folded into a new suite of methods and pathways. A stakeholder-feedback window on the supporting resources closed 31 July 2026; a final resource set is expected Q4 2026, target-setting under V2.0 opens 1 February 2027, and the validation portal opens Q1 2027. Separately, on 15 July 2026 SBTi opened a second consultation on the Power Sector Net-Zero Standard.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly frames the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target and is the standard that governs how vPPA/REC/REV procurement counts toward net-zero — bearing on Valle 4 (300 GWh/yr), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO instrument. V1.3 remains valid for new targets until end-2027, so no immediate re-basing, but the treatment of market-based instruments and the new ongoing-responsibility mechanism must be read against the current procurement strategy.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/blog/the-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-is-here-what-comes-next

## EU ETS — post-2030 review proposal published [NEW]

- **Source/date:** European Commission — ETS review proposal published 17 July 2026 (part of the Electrification Action Plan / Clean Industrial Deal package).
- **What changed:** The baseline expected a broader ETS revision around Q1 2027; the Commission has instead published the post-2030 (Phase 5–6) proposal early, on 17 July 2026, to align the system with the 2040 target. Headline elements: a steeper Linear Reduction Factor (4.4% from 2028, 3.7% for 2031–2035, 1.7% from 2036); a €100bn Industrial Decarbonisation Bank with a 2028 "Investment Booster"; the Market Stability Reserve intake rate cut from 24% to 12%; and a requirement that member states earmark at least 50% of auction revenues for decarbonisation. Council/Parliament target political agreement by Q1 2027, with debate intensifying from September 2026.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the renewable-electricity instruments (vPPA/REC/REV). Relevant only as a forward carbon-cost signal across the EU value chain and Spanish operations, feeding Scope 3 input costs.
- **Link:** https://malta.representation.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-boosts-europes-competitiveness-decarbonisation-and-independence-electrification-action-2026-07-17_en

## GHG Protocol — corporate standards consolidation with ISO [NEW DIRECTION]

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Consolidated Standard Development Plan published 29 July 2026.
- **What changed:** GHGP and ISO have confirmed they are aligning around a single, co-branded corporate standard. This will consolidate the Corporate Standard (2004), Scope 2 Guidance (2015), Scope 3 Standard (2011) and the Actions & Market Instruments workstream with ISO 14064-1, via an integrated public consultation planned for Q2 2027. The AMI workstream reported ~500 responses to its Request for Information (closed 31 May 2026), with a feedback overview due in the coming months. This reframes the previously separate Scope 2, Scope 3 and AMI revision tracks the baseline was following as one merged programme.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Bears on where the REV GoO instrument and the vPPA/REC volumes are credited in Haleon's GHG report. The AMI multi-statement reporting model (market-based inventory + impact statement) now sits inside a single consolidated standard, so the accounting treatment of all market instruments will be decided in one Q2 2027 consultation rather than three — a single window to influence and prepare for.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Consolidated-StandardDevelopmentPlan(SDP)-2026.07.29.pdf

## GHG Protocol — Scope 2 Guidance revision (timeline only)

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — second consultation still expected in 2026; final 2027.
- **What changed:** No new draft since the baseline. The Market-Based Accounting Technical Working Group continues to examine hourly/granular matching (likely to become the floor), deliverability tightening and the legacy-contract question. A second public consultation covering Scope 2/3 interactions and steam/heat/cooling is expected later in 2026. Now folded into the ISO consolidation above.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Unchanged from baseline — the deliverability test, hourly matching and legacy clause remain the decisive factors for whether Valle 4, the Texas deal and the REV instrument stay claimable under the market-based method.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-2-guidance

## GHG Protocol — Scope 3 Standard revision (timeline only)

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Phase 1 Progress Update (31 March 2026) still the latest output.
- **What changed:** No new publication. The full public-consultation draft, previously expected mid-2026, has slipped to the second half of 2026 and may be aligned with the ISO integration timetable. Proposals unchanged: ≥95% coverage (≤5% exclusions), tiered data disaggregation, new Category 16.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Unchanged — a 95% coverage rule and tiered data disclosure would raise the data-quality bar for supplier-electricity reporting, including the REV GoO volumes, against the 42% Scope 3 target.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-3-standard-revisions-phase-1-progress-update

## CBAM — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (background)

- **Source/date:** European Commission — no material change since the December 2025 implementation package.
- **What changed:** Nothing substantive since the baseline. The definitive regime remains live from 1 January 2026; the downstream-scope extension proposal and anti-circumvention measures continue through the legislative process. From 2027 the certificate price mirrors the weekly average ETS price.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the electricity instruments. Watch only for input-cost effects on packaging/steel/aluminium supply chains feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en

## Fit for 55 (background)

- **Source/date:** European Commission — 2040 target and GHG-removals track.
- **What changed:** The European Parliament approved a binding 2040 target (90% net GHG reduction vs 1990) in February 2026. Findings from the consultation on integrating GHG removals were expected by July 2026 and feed the ETS review above. No new Fit-for-55 package.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Background context only; no direct effect on the PPAs, REV instrument or Scope 3 target.
- **Link:** https://commission.europa.eu/topics/climate-action/delivering-european-green-deal/fit-55-delivering-proposals_en
