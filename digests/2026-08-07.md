# Reg watch — 7 August 2026

Two material developments since the 5 June baseline: the SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 is now published (the June priority-watch item), and the European Commission has tabled its post-2030 EU ETS review. GHG Protocol Scope 2, Scope 3 and AMI workstreams have not produced new output this period.

## LEAD — SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 published [PRIORITY WATCH cleared]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 published 11 June 2026; feedback window on the accompanying implementation resources closed 31 July 2026.
- **What changed:** SBTi's June priority-watch item has landed. V2 shifts the centre of gravity from ambition-setting to implementation accountability — transition plans, an ongoing-emissions-responsibility framework and periodic progress assessment are now embedded in the Standard itself, with revised Scope 2/3 treatment. Existing sector guidance and pathways (incl. chemicals) remain valid to end-January 2028 and have been folded into the V2 methods-and-pathways suite; final implementation resources are due Q4 2026, and V2 becomes mandatory for new targets from 2028.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly frames the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target and — through the revised Scope 2/3 treatment and ongoing-emissions rules — how vPPA, REC and REV (GoO retirement) procurement is allowed to count toward net-zero. This is the item that most warrants a full read before Q4 2026, when the interpretation resources finalise.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/blog/the-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-is-here-what-comes-next

## EU ETS — post-2030 review proposal tabled

- **Source/date:** European Commission — EU ETS revision proposal published 17 July 2026, alongside a new Electrification Action Plan.
- **What changed:** The Commission tabled its long-trailed post-2030 ETS overhaul. Headline measures: the linear reduction factor rises to 4.4% (2028), eases to 3.7% (2031–35) then 1.7% from 2036; free allocation for heavy industry is prolonged into the 2040s, and the CBAM-linked free-allocation phase-out is slowed and extended to 2038 (from 2034); the Market Stability Reserve intake rate is halved to 12%; a €100bn Industrial Decarbonisation Bank is created; and Member States must earmark ≥50% of auction revenue for ETS-sector decarbonisation. Council/Parliament target political agreement by Q1 2027; debate intensifies from September 2026.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments, but a material carbon-cost signal across the EU value chain and Spanish operations feeding Scope 3; the paired Electrification Action Plan is worth a light watch for any measures touching corporate PPAs.
- **Link:** https://www.globalelr.com/2026/07/european-commission-publishes-eu-ets-review-proposing-significant-reforms/

## GHG Protocol — Scope 2 Guidance revision

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — no new publication since the Oct 2025–Jan 2026 consultation.
- **What changed:** No material change this period. Finalised revised text on the key Scope 2 requirements (updated location-based emission-factor hierarchy; market-based hourly-matching and deliverability requirements) is still expected around mid-2026, with a second public consultation on remaining topics to follow later in 2026 and final publication end-2027. The legacy clause for existing contracts and hourly-matching exemption thresholds remain in the proposals.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Unchanged from baseline — the deliverability and hourly-matching tests will determine whether the Valle 4 vPPA (300 GWh/yr, 2027–2036), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO retirement stay claimable under the market-based method; the legacy clause is the key protection. Watch for the finalised text.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/scope-2-standard-advances-isb-approves-consultation-market-and-location-based-revisions

## GHG Protocol — Scope 3 Standard revision

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — no new output since the Phase 1 Progress Update (31 March 2026).
- **What changed:** No material change. The full public-consultation draft is still expected in H2 2026, though it may be re-timed to align with the ISO–GHG Protocol integration work; final standard remains targeted for late 2027. Core proposals (high coverage, mandatory disaggregation by data source type, Category 15/16 changes) are unchanged.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Bears on the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target — tiered data-quality and coverage rules would raise the bar for supplier-electricity reporting, including volumes covered by the REV GoO instrument. No action until the consultation draft appears.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-3-standard-revisions-phase-1-progress-update

## GHG Protocol — Actions and Market Instruments (AMI)

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — RFI closed 31 May 2026; no new output.
- **What changed:** No material change. The 60-day RFI on a multi-statement reporting structure (physical inventory plus a market-based inventory and a GHG impact statement) has closed; draft standard still planned Q3 2027, final ~2028.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly relevant to the REV instrument and vPPA/REC reporting — the multi-statement model would change where these are credited — but nothing to act on until a draft emerges.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/ghg-protocol-launches-action-and-market-instruments-request-information-what-it-and-why-it

## CBAM — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism

- **Source/date:** European Commission — no new act since December 2025; downstream-scope proposal in ordinary legislative procedure.
- **What changed:** No material CBAM-specific change this period. The December 2025 proposal to extend scope to ~180 downstream steel/aluminium products (plus anti-circumvention measures) is still passing through co-decision. The most relevant recent move sits under the ETS proposal above: the CBAM free-allocation phase-out is now slated to run to 2038 rather than 2034.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the electricity instruments; watch only for input-cost effects on packaging/steel/aluminium supply chains feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/cbam-legislation-and-guidance_en
