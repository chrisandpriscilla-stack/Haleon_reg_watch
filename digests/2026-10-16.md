# Reg watch — 16 October 2026

Two material developments since the 28 August digest, both in the EU legislative pipeline and both indirect to Haleon: the post-2030 EU ETS review has reached its first Environment Council policy debate with the Presidency now pushing for agreement before year-end, and the European Parliament has adopted its CBAM scope-expansion mandate, sending it into trilogue. No change to the items that bear directly on Haleon's instruments — SBTi CNZS V2, the GHG Protocol consolidated standard and the Scope 3 revision are all unchanged since late August (SBTi's final V2 implementation resources remain due this quarter).

## EU ETS — post-2030 review reaches Council; agreement now targeted for December 2026

- **Source/date:** Environment Council policy debate, 12 October 2026 (proposal tabled 17 July 2026, file 2026/0212 (COD)).
- **What changed:** The post-2030 ETS review (Phases 5 & 6) had its first ministerial debate. Ministers discussed cap ambition, international credits and permanent removals, use of auction revenue, free allocation and carbon-leakage, and scope extensions (aviation, maritime, waste). The Presidency signalled it is aiming for a Council general approach **by December 2026** — materially earlier than the Q1 2027 political-agreement timeline flagged in August. The proposal's substance is unchanged (LRF cut to 3.7% for 2031–2035 and 1.7% from 2036; €100bn Industrial Decarbonisation Bank; ≥50% auction-revenue earmarking; MSR intake halved 24%→12%; municipal-waste incineration in scope from 2031; set-aside for international credits and domestic removals).
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the Valle 4 vPPA, the Texas solar deal, the REV instrument or the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target. Relevant only as an EU-wide carbon-cost signal across the value chain and Spanish operations feeding Scope 3; the accelerated timeline means the post-2030 cost trajectory could firm up sooner than assumed.
- **Link:** https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/meetings/env/2026/10/12/

## CBAM — Parliament adopts scope-expansion mandate; first trilogue 20 October 2026

- **Source/date:** European Parliament plenary vote, 15 September 2026 (464–50, 159 abstentions).
- **What changed:** Parliament adopted its first-reading negotiating mandate on the CBAM scope-extension regulation, backing extension to 400+ **downstream steel and aluminium products from 2028** and dropping the Commission's price-shock "safety valve"; a companion Temporary Decarbonisation Fund report also passed (433–97). With the Council's general approach already agreed (12 June 2026), the **first political trilogue is scheduled for 20 October 2026**. Separately, the consultation on the draft delegated regulation for the sale/repurchase of CBAM certificates (opened 9 July) closed 6 August 2026; final product counts remain open pending trilogue.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments. Watch only for input-cost effects on steel, aluminium and packaging feeding Scope 3; a wider downstream list from 2028 would broaden that exposure, but nothing is binding until a final text is agreed.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en

## No change this period — items bearing directly on Haleon

- **SBTi CNZS V2:** No new publication since 28 August. Final priority implementation resources remain due **Q4 2026** (Renewal Policy expected this quarter; Ongoing Emissions Responsibility framework in 2027); validation against V2 opens **1 February 2027**, with V1.3.1 available in the interim. Nothing new to report, but the Q4 window is now open — the deep-dive below is time-sensitive.
- **GHG Protocol (Scope 2 / Scope 3 / Actions & Market Instruments):** Unchanged. The consolidated, ISO-partnered Corporate Standard remains on the 29 July SDP track — single public-consultation draft expected **Q2 2027**, final ~2028. No interim drafts published.
- **Fit for 55 / EU 2040 framework:** Unchanged. The 90%-by-2040 target is in force; delivery runs through the ETS review above. No new delegated acts bearing on Haleon.
