## Material change this week

### SBTi — Forest, Land and Agriculture (FLAG) Standard revision: call for input opened
**Source:** Science Based Targets initiative, early September 2026.
The SBTi has opened a call for input to revise its FLAG Standard, the first step toward realigning land-sector target-setting with the Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 (absolute contraction approach, "best efforts" framing) and the new GHG Protocol Land Sector and Removals Standard. Expression of interest to join stakeholder meetings closes **8 September 2026**; the call for evidence closes **8 October 2026**. Interim guidance is unchanged: FLAG companies continue applying the current standard while drawing on V2 innovations.

**Relevance to Haleon:** Tangential to the four tracked instruments. FLAG governs land-sector emissions (agriculture, forestry) rather than electricity, so it does not touch the Valle 4 Spain vPPA, the Texas solar tax-credit transfer, or the REV GoO instrument. It bears only indirectly on the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target, and only to the extent Haleon carries material FLAG-category emissions from agricultural/botanical ingredients or fibre-based packaging — Haleon is not a FLAG-designated company for its core target-setting. Monitor, no action.

**Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/news/sbti-issues-call-for-input-to-inform-revision-of-forest-land-and-agriculture-standard
