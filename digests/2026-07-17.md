# Reg watch — 17 July 2026

Two material developments this week: the European Commission's post-2030 EU ETS revision proposal was published today, and the SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 has moved into its implementation phase with a stakeholder feedback window closing 31 July. Prior baselines (5 June) were diffed; only substantive changes are surfaced below.

## LEAD — EU ETS, post-2030 revision proposal published today

- **Source/date:** European Commission — ETS revision proposal published 17 July 2026.
- **What changed:** The Commission tabled its long-trailed post-2030 ETS reform (previously expected only in Q1 2027). It keeps the three-tier free-allocation structure but tightens conditionality — larger free-permit volumes for industry in exchange for binding EU decarbonisation investment commitments — and, for the first time, sets out integration of permanent carbon removals into the ETS and recognition of post-2040 residual emissions, aligned to the EU's 2040 target of a 90% net cut. Published alongside an Electrification Action Plan, a CORSIA assessment, and new 2026–30 sector benchmarks extending free allowances to further industries.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the Valle 4 vPPA, the Texas Solar PV deal or the REV GoO instrument. Matters as an EU carbon-cost signal across the value chain and Spanish operations; the Electrification Action Plan is the strand worth tracking for demand-side/renewables policy that could touch PPA economics.
- **Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1044

## SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 [PRIORITY WATCH]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — V2.0 published June 2026; implementation resources rolling out through 2026.
- **What changed:** The priority-watch item has resolved: V2.0 is final. The initiative is now consulting on the new target-setting methods and sector pathways that operationalise it — **stakeholder feedback is open until 31 July 2026**, with final resources due Q4 2026. V1.3.1 remains the applicable framework for validations throughout 2026; from Q1 2027 companies may submit under either V1.3.1 or V2.0 during a transition period. V2.0 shifts emphasis from target-setting to implementation, with revised Scope 2 and Scope 3 treatment, an Ongoing Emissions Responsibility (OER) framework, and updated removals/assurance provisions.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly frames the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target and could reshape how vPPA/REC/REV procurement counts toward net-zero. The revised Scope 2 treatment is the key line for the Valle 4 and Texas deals; the OER framework and Scope 3 rules bear on the REV GoO instrument. The 31 July feedback window is a near-term opportunity to influence the pathways.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/news/the-sbti-releases-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-to-accelerate-corporate-climate-action

## GHG Protocol — Scope 2 Guidance revision

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Independent Standards Board (ISB) approved a second consultation on the market- and location-based revisions.
- **What changed:** Incremental progress against the previously reported timeline. The ISB has cleared a second Scope 2 public consultation (2026) covering the updated location-based emission-factor hierarchy and the market-based changes already flagged — hourly (time-matched) accounting, a deliverability/geographic-matching requirement, and, critically, the legacy clause for long-term contracts plus a smaller-organisation exemption. Final standard still targeted 2027.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** The standing deep-dive item. The deliverability test and hourly matching remain the decisive factors for whether Valle 4 (300 GWh/yr), Texas Solar PV (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO retirement stay claimable under the market-based method; the legacy clause is the protection to track into the second consultation.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/scope-2-standard-advances-isb-approves-consultation-market-and-location-based-revisions

## CBAM — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism

- **Source/date:** European Commission — consultation opened 9 July 2026 (feedback due 6 August 2026).
- **What changed:** Operational, not structural. The Commission opened a consultation on a draft delegated regulation governing how the sale and repurchase of CBAM certificates will work in the definitive regime. The wider scope extension to ~180 downstream steel- and aluminium-intensive products (implementation 1 Jan 2028) remains as previously reported.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the electricity instruments. Watch only for input-cost effects on packaging/steel/aluminium feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en
