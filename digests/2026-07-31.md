## LEAD — SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 PUBLISHED

- **Source/date:** SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 published 11 June 2026.
- **What changed:** The final V2.0 is now out (baseline had it as "not yet published"). Structurally, market instruments — PPAs, RECs, GoOs — are **no longer bundled into the Scope 2 target itself**; they sit under a separate implementation hierarchy. Companies must set a location-based Scope 2 reduction target plus *either* a market-based reduction target *or* a zero-carbon electricity target. Eligible instruments explicitly include **physical and virtual PPAs**, supplier low-carbon contracts and unbundled EACs, but V2 introduces new **spatial matching and (where possible) time-matching** criteria for the EACs that can be claimed. Implementation resources are in draft with a stakeholder feedback window closing **today, 31 July 2026**; final resources due Q4 2026. V1.3.1 remains the only validation route through 2026; V2.0 opens for target-setting **1 February 2027** (transition period, either version, from Q1 2027).
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly bears on the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target *and* on how the Valle 4 vPPA (300 GWh/yr), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO instrument count toward net-zero. vPPAs remain eligible, which is reassuring, but the new spatial/time-matching criteria for EACs are the exposure to track — particularly for the REV GoO retirement on supplier electricity, where geographic and temporal matching may not currently hold. The 31 July feedback deadline is closing as this runs.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/news/the-sbti-releases-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-to-accelerate-corporate-climate-action

## EU ETS — Post-2030 revision proposal tabled

- **Source/date:** European Commission — EU ETS revision proposal published 17 July 2026.
- **What changed:** Tabled earlier than the Q1 2027 timing flagged at baseline. Linear Reduction Factor rises to 4.4% in 2028, eases to 3.7% (2031–35) and 1.7% from 2036, pushing the effective "endgame" from ~2039 to ~2048. Free allocation for heavy industry is **prolonged past 2030 into the 2040s**, with an immediate ~€6bn (80 Mt) boost via revised 2026–2030 fallback benchmarks; the Market Stability Reserve intake rate is cut from 24% to 12%; and member states must earmark ≥50% of auction revenue for decarbonisation. The proposal also opens an indirect pathway for permanent removals (DACCS/Bio-CCS) — which discharges the Fit for 55 negative-emissions report due 31 July 2026. Council/Parliament target political agreement by Q1 2027.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the renewable-electricity instruments. Relevant as a carbon-cost signal across EU value-chain inputs and Spanish operations; a slower endgame and prolonged free allocation modestly soften the near-term EU carbon-price trajectory feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/eu-commission-publishes-eu-ets-review-proposal

## GHG Protocol — Scope 2 Guidance revision

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — revision ongoing; final now targeted late 2027.
- **What changed:** Timeline and status refined since baseline. The final output is now expected **late 2027 and is to be issued as a Standard, not merely Guidance**; a second public consultation is still expected during 2026 (adding Scope 2/3 interactions and purchased heat/steam/cooling). The three market-based-method proposals are confirmed as an **hourly matching requirement, a deliverability requirement, and a new "Marginal Emissions Impact" metric**.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Remains the structural watch item for the Valle 4 vPPA, the Texas deal and REV GoO. Elevation from Guidance to Standard raises its future weight; the deliverability and hourly-matching tests still determine whether these volumes stay claimable under the market-based method. No action yet — draft still pending.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/upcoming-scope-2-public-consultation-overview-revisions

## GHG Protocol — Scope 3 Standard revision

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Phase 1 Progress Update (31 March 2026); no new output since.
- **What changed:** No new publication. The full public-consultation draft (previously "mid-2026") is now flagged as possibly **slipping into H2 2026 to align with ISO–GHG Protocol integration work**. Proposals unchanged: ≥95% coverage, tiered data disaggregation, new Category 16.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Bears on the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target; a 95% coverage rule and tiered data would raise the bar for supplier-emissions data, including REV-covered electricity. Watch for the consultation draft.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/scope-3-standard-revisions-phase-1-progress-update

## CBAM — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism

- **Source/date:** European Commission — definitive regime live since 1 Jan 2026.
- **What changed:** Compliance phase running (4,100+ authorised declarants). Default values escalate +10% (2026), +20% (2027), +30% (2028+); certificate sales begin 1 Feb 2027; the simplified threshold exempts ~90% of importers while covering 99% of embedded emissions. A scope/default-value review runs through 2027.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments. Input-cost watch only for packaging/steel/aluminium in Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en
