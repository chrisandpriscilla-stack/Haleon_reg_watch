# Reg watch — 25 September 2026

One genuinely new material development since the 28 August digest: the Council has agreed its negotiating position on the fast-track measure to increase free EU ETS allowances for energy-intensive sectors. Elsewhere, only a firming of already-known SBTi and GHG Protocol timelines. No new standards, drafts or consultations were published this week.

## LEAD — EU ETS: Council agrees position on more free allowances for energy-intensive sectors

- **Source/date:** Council of the EU (Coreper) — press release, 16 September 2026.
- **What changed:** Member States (at ambassador level) agreed the Council's position on the Commission's July 2026 fast-track amendment to raise free ETS allowances for sectors under the heat and fuel benchmarks over **2026–2030**. It mobilises ~88m allowances already available for free allocation plus a further ~33m previously unallocated, worth roughly **€6bn** in cost relief, to offset the tighter benchmarks set in June. This is a separate, near-term carbon-leakage measure, not the broader post-2030 ETS review (still targeting political agreement Q1 2027); the Council is now ready to open trilogue with Parliament.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the Valle 4 vPPA, the Texas Solar PV deal or the REV GoO instrument. Relevant only as a carbon-cost signal across the EU/Spanish value chain feeding Scope 3 — more free allocation slightly softens near-term EU carbon costs for upstream industrial inputs (steel, aluminium, packaging).
- **Link:** https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/09/16/ets-council-agrees-targeted-measure-to-protect-energy-intensive-sectors-from-carbon-leakage/

## SBTi — V2.0 go-live firmed to 1 February 2027 [standing PRIORITY WATCH]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — "What comes next" guidance on CNZS V2.0.
- **What changed:** No new standard, but the timeline has firmed. Version 2.0 will open for target-setting from **1 February 2027**, released with a full suite of methods, pathways, FAQs and a beta target-setting tool. **V1.3.1 and the existing sector guidance (incl. chemicals, FLAG) now remain valid until end of January 2028**, giving a defined transition runway. V2 carries an **implementation hierarchy** (direct reductions → market instruments) and a "best efforts" provision — the mechanics that govern how PPA/REC/REV procurement will count.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Central and unchanged as the key open question. The implementation hierarchy is exactly where the Valle 4 vPPA, Texas Solar PV deal and REV GoO instrument will be ranked for credit against the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target under V2. The firmer 1 Feb 2027 go-live and the end-Jan 2028 V1.3.1 sunset set the window in which to pin down eligibility before switching.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/blog/the-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-is-here-what-comes-next

## GHG Protocol — consolidated (ISO) standard: process only, no new output

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — standard-development updates / Scope 2 TWG.
- **What changed:** No draft published. The Scope 2 Technical Working Group held an in-person meeting in September to reconcile the ~1,100 comments from the first consultation. The consolidated Corporate Standard (merging Scope 1/2/3 and AMI with ISO 14064-1) remains on track for a single **public consultation in Q2 2027**, with final publication now stated as **Q4 2028**. The deliverability/hourly-matching and EAC quality tests (vintage, additionality, deliverability) that determine PPA/GoO claimability continue to move through this one process.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Central over the medium term but no action this week. The rules that decide whether the vPPA/Texas/REV instruments remain claimable under the market-based method are still being drafted; the Q2 2027 consultation is the influence point.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/ghg-protocol-announces-key-standard-development-updates
