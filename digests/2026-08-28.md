# Reg watch — 28 August 2026

Three material developments since the 5 June baseline: SBTi's Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 is now published, the GHG Protocol has consolidated its Scope 2 / Scope 3 / market-instruments workstreams into a single ISO-partnered standard, and the Commission has tabled its post-2030 EU ETS review. Detail below.

## LEAD — GHG Protocol consolidates Scope 2, Scope 3 and AMI into a single ISO-partnered standard

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Consolidated Corporate Standard Development Plan, 29 July 2026.
- **What changed:** GHGP has announced that the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance, Scope 3 Standard and the Actions & Market Instruments workstream will be merged into **one harmonised corporate GHG accounting standard, developed in partnership with ISO** (integrating ISO 14064-1). The separate tracks tracked in June — a Scope 2 "second consultation" in 2026, a Scope 3 consultation mid-2026, an AMI draft in Q3 2027 — are superseded. A single **consolidated draft is now expected for public consultation in Q2 2027**, with final publication thereafter. The substantive Scope 2 proposals (hourly/time-matched accounting, a deliverability/geographic-matching test, the legacy clause for existing contracts) and the Scope 3 proposals (≥95% coverage, tiered data, Category 16) carry forward into this single process.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Central. The deliverability test and hourly-matching rules that will determine whether the Valle 4 Spain vPPA (300 GWh/yr, 2027–2036), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO instrument remain claimable under the market-based method are now on a single, later timeline (consultation Q2 2027) — more runway to influence, but the legacy-clause protection and where market instruments are credited are now decided in one place rather than three.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Consolidated-StandardDevelopmentPlan(SDP)-2026.07.29.pdf

## SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 PUBLISHED [PRIORITY WATCH cleared]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — CNZS V2.0 published 11 June 2026.
- **What changed:** The final V2.0 is out (the June baseline was still awaiting it). Implementation and progress-assessment guidance is being released through 2026; the stakeholder-feedback window on draft implementation resources **closed 31 July 2026**, with final priority resources due **Q4 2026** before validations open. Companies can submit targets against V2 for validation from **early 2027**; V1.3.1 remains available in the interim.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly frames the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target and, critically, how vPPA/REC/REV procurement counts toward net-zero under V2's revised Scope 2/3 treatment and ongoing-emissions provisions. Now actionable rather than a watch item — the interpretation of market-instrument eligibility under V2 is the key question to pin down.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/corporate-net-zero-standard-v2

## EU ETS — post-2030 review proposal published (17 July 2026)

- **Source/date:** European Commission — EU ETS review proposal, 17 July 2026.
- **What changed:** The flagged July proposal is now tabled. It cuts the Linear Reduction Factor to 3.7% (2031–2035) and 1.7% from 2036, sets up a €100bn Industrial Decarbonisation Bank (Phase 1 from 2028), requires Member States to earmark ≥50% of auction revenue for decarbonisation, halves the Market Stability Reserve intake rate (24%→12%), and opens the door to permanent domestic carbon removals. Political agreement is targeted for Q1 2027.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the renewable-electricity instruments. Relevant only as a carbon-cost signal across the EU value chain and Spanish operations feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/eu-commission-publishes-eu-ets-review-proposal

## CBAM — operational guidance and certificate-trading rules

- **Source/date:** European Commission — draft delegated regulation consultation (9 July 2026); 10 guidance documents (14 Aug 2026).
- **What changed:** Implementation detail, not new scope. The Commission opened a consultation on how CBAM certificates are sold and repurchased, and published four general plus six sector guidance documents (incl. iron & steel, aluminium). The downstream scope-extension consultation closed 10 June.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments; watch only for input-cost effects on steel/aluminium/packaging in Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en

## Fit for 55 / EU 2040 framework

- **Source/date:** European Commission — 90% 2040 target in force (April 2026); GHG-removals findings due July 2026.
- **What changed:** The legally binding 90%-by-2040 net-reduction target (Climate Law amendment) is in force, with limited use of international credits and a route for permanent removals via the ETS. Delivered largely through the 17 July ETS review above.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Background context; no direct effect on the PPAs, REV instrument or the Scope 3 target.
- **Link:** https://commission.europa.eu/topics/climate-action/delivering-european-green-deal/fit-55-delivering-proposals_en
