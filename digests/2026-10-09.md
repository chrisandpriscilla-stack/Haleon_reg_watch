# Reg watch — 9 October 2026

One material development since the 28 August snapshot: the EU ETS post-2030 review has moved from Commission proposal into the European Parliament's negotiating phase, with the lead MEP's draft report now tabled. The tracks that actually govern Haleon's instruments — SBTi V2 implementation guidance and the GHG Protocol's ISO-consolidated standard — have not moved; both remain on their previously reported Q4 2026 / Q2 2027 timelines. One minor date refinement on SBTi noted below.

## EU ETS — Parliament enters negotiation; rapporteur's draft report tabled

- **Source/date:** European Parliament (ENVI) — rapporteur Peter Liese (EPP) draft report, presented early September 2026 (Carbon Pulse, 10 Sept; S&P Global, 11 Sept 2026).
- **What changed:** The post-2030 ETS review (Commission proposal of 17 July 2026) has entered the co-decision phase. Peter Liese, named Parliament rapporteur in mid-July, has tabled a draft report that would **slow the annual pace of emission cuts after 2030** (softening the Commission's Linear Reduction Factor path), **strengthen the price-stability safeguards** against sharp carbon-price swings, propose a **phase-in period** for the new rules, and seek an end to certain derogations. Ireland's Council presidency is targeting a deal by end-2026, a timeline Liese himself called "ambitious."
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Indirect only. No bearing on the Valle 4 Spain vPPA, the Texas Solar PV deal or the REV GoO instrument. Relevant as a forward carbon-cost signal across the EU value chain and Spanish operations feeding Scope 3 — a Parliament push to soften the cap trajectory would, if it holds, moderate the EU carbon-price path versus the Commission's July proposal.
- **Link:** https://carbon-pulse.com/549270/

## SBTi — no new resources; validation window refined to 1 February 2027 [PRIORITY WATCH]

- **Source/date:** SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 (published 11 June 2026); ongoing implementation rollout.
- **What changed:** No new implementation guidance, FAQ or sector pathway has been published since the stakeholder-feedback window closed 31 July 2026. SBTi's own materials now give the V2 validation open date as **1 February 2027** (the 28 Aug snapshot said "early 2027" — this is a firmer date, not a new event). The Renewal Policy and remaining priority implementation resources are still flagged for **Q4 2026**, i.e. imminent but not yet out; Ongoing Emission Responsibility launches in 2027. V1.3.1 remains usable for new targets in the interim.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Central, unchanged. The treatment of vPPA/REC/REV procurement toward the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target hinges on the Q4 2026 implementation resources. This remains the item most worth pre-positioning for before those resources land.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/blog/the-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-is-here-what-comes-next

## GHG Protocol (Scope 2 / Scope 3 / AMI consolidation) — no change

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Consolidated Corporate Standard Development Plan, 29 July 2026.
- **What changed:** Nothing new since the SDP. The consolidated, ISO-partnered single standard (integrating Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance, Scope 3 Standard and AMI with ISO 14064-1) remains on track for an integrated public consultation in **Q2 2027**. No draft text, no interim consultation in Sept/Oct.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Central but static. The deliverability / hourly-matching tests and the legacy clause that will decide whether the vPPA, Texas deal and REV GoO instrument stay claimable under the market-based method are still being decided in this single Q2 2027 process. Runway to influence; no action forced this week.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/updates-ghg-protocol-actions-and-market-instruments-workstream

## CBAM / Fit for 55 — no material change

- **Source/date:** European Commission — CBAM and 2040 framework pages.
- **What changed:** Nothing new. CBAM: no new legal act or guidance since the ten guidance documents of 14 Aug 2026 and the certificate sale/repurchase consultation that closed early August. Fit for 55 / 2040: the 90%-by-2040 Climate Law amendment remains in force (since 7 April 2026); no new implementing package tabled in Sept/Oct.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the electricity instruments. CBAM relevant only for input-cost effects on steel/aluminium/packaging feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en
