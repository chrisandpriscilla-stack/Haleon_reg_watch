# Reg watch — 21 August 2026

First diff run against the 5 June 2026 baseline (a ~2.5-month gap, so several substantive developments have accumulated). Material changes only below.

## LEAD — GHG Protocol, Scope 2 revision: consultation feedback published

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — feedback summary published 29 July 2026.
- **What changed:** GHGP released its summary of the Scope 2 public consultation (1,100+ comments). Notably, there is **weak stakeholder support for mandatory hourly (time-matched) accounting**, with respondents pressing for a more flexible approach than the draft proposed. The technical working group meets in September to reconcile comments; GHGP is also reassessing the "consequential" reporting rules. A second consultation is expected later in 2026 (adding Scope 2/3 interactions and steam/heat/cooling), with the final update now slated for **late 2027** (previously framed as final methodology 2027 / implementation 2028 — a modest slip).
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Directly bears on the Valle 4 Spain vPPA (300 GWh/yr, 2027–2036), the Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr) and the REV GoO instrument. Weak support for compulsory hourly matching is a **favourable early signal** — annual matching is more likely to survive — but the deliverability/geographic-matching test and the legacy clause remain the decisive open questions.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/upcoming-scope-2-public-consultation-overview-revisions

## SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 now PUBLISHED

- **Source/date:** SBTi — Version 2.0 published 11 June 2026.
- **What changed:** The final V2.0 is out (the baseline recorded it as unpublished as at 5 June). It merges the Near-Term Criteria and the Net-Zero Standard into one framework and shifts emphasis from target-setting to **implementation accountability** — mandatory transition plans, an Ongoing Emissions Responsibility (OER) framework, regular progress assessment, and revised Scope 2/3 and removals treatment. Transition: V1.3.1 remains the applicable framework for validation throughout 2026; from Q1 2027 companies may submit under either V1.3.1 or V2.0.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Frames the 42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target and reshapes how vPPA/REC/REV procurement counts toward net-zero. The revised Scope 2 guidance and OER framework are the passages to read for how Haleon's market instruments are credited. No filing action in 2026, but V2 alignment planning should begin ahead of the 2027 window.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/corporate-net-zero-standard-v2

## EU ETS — post-2030 revision proposal tabled

- **Source/date:** European Commission — proposal published 17 July 2026.
- **What changed:** The Commission tabled its EU ETS review for Phases 5–6 (post-2030), under the Clean Industrial Deal. It lowers the cap's Linear Reduction Factor to **3.7% for 2031–2035 and 1.7% from 2036**, aligning with the 90%-by-2040 net target and pushing the "zero-cap endgame" from ~2039 to ~2048. From 2031, free allocation becomes **fully conditional** on a verified decarbonisation investment plan (80% on approval, 20% on demonstrated reductions per five-year period). A parallel benchmarks proposal adds ~€6bn of free allocation to industry for 2026–2030.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the renewable-electricity instruments (vPPA/REC/REV). Relevant only as a carbon-cost signal across the EU value chain and Spanish operations; conditional free allocation may filter into supplier input costs feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1044

## CBAM — Council agrees position on downstream scope extension

- **Source/date:** European Council — general approach agreed 12 June 2026.
- **What changed:** The Council agreed its negotiating position on the December 2025 proposal to extend CBAM to ~180 downstream steel- and aluminium-intensive products, with anti-circumvention measures; the extension would take effect **1 January 2028** once Parliament and Council conclude trilogues. (Baseline had the 13 May implementing act and 10 June consultation; this is legislative progression.)
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the electricity instruments. Watch only for input-cost effects on packaging/steel/aluminium supply chains feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/06/12/council-moves-to-strengthen-the-eu-s-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/
