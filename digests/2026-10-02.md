# Reg watch — 2 October 2026

Two material developments since the 28 August digest. SBTi has released its first V2.0 validation resources (1 October) and confirmed that the guidance governing how renewable-electricity instruments count under the new standard — market-based-instrument accounting rules and the Ongoing Emissions Responsibility (OER) recognition framework — lands this quarter (Q4 2026). Separately, the EU Council agreed its negotiating position on the targeted Market Stability Reserve amendment (23 September). GHG Protocol (consolidated/ISO track), CBAM and Fit for 55 show only incremental, non-material movement.

## LEAD — SBTi releases first V2.0 validation resources; market-instrument and OER rules due Q4 2026

- **Source/date:** SBTi / SBTi Services — V2.0 validation resources released **1 October 2026**; forward roadmap for Q4 2026.
- **What changed:** SBTi Services published the first batch of **Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0 validation resources on 1 October 2026** — the first concrete step toward validations opening (targets can be submitted against V2 from early 2027; V2 is effective for validation from 1 February 2027). Critically for procurement, SBTi has confirmed that by the end of 2026 it will publish **final target-setting methodologies and pathways, additional interpretation guidance, preliminary guidance for the accounting and reporting of market-based instruments, and an initial recognition framework for instruments eligible under the Ongoing Emissions Responsibility (OER) programme** (the instrument-recognition process is slated for Q4 2026; the associated Claims Policy for Q1 2027). The published V2 text confirms two substantive rules of direct relevance: eligible market instruments must come from **generators commissioned or re-powered within the preceding 15 years**, and the eligible-instrument list **explicitly includes physical and virtual PPAs, supplier low-carbon-energy contracts and unbundled energy attribute certificates**. OER operates on three tiers (Engaged ≥1% of ongoing Scope 1–3; Advanced; Leadership).
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Central, and now time-critical. The Q4 2026 market-instrument and OER recognition guidance is the document that will determine whether the **Valle 4 Spain vPPA (300 GWh/yr, 2027–2036)**, the **Texas Solar PV deal (100 GWh/yr)** and the **REV Scope 3 GoO-retirement instrument** are recognised under V2 and how they count toward the **42%-by-2030 Scope 3 target**. The confirmed 15-year commissioning/re-powering test and the explicit inclusion of vPPAs are favourable signals for the two new-build PPAs; the REV GoO/unbundled-EAC treatment is the open question the Q4 guidance should answer. This should be pinned down before the guidance is finalised.
- **Link:** https://sciencebasedtargets.org/blog/the-corporate-net-zero-standard-v2-0-is-here-what-comes-next

## EU ETS — Council agrees position on targeted Market Stability Reserve amendment (23 September)

- **Source/date:** Council of the EU (Consilium) — MSR targeted amendment, Coreper agreement **23 September 2026**.
- **What changed:** Member States' ambassadors agreed the Council's negotiating position on the targeted MSR amendment. The Council wants to **retain the mechanism that cancels surplus allowances in the Reserve until end-2030** (the Commission had proposed indefinite suspension) and to **raise the automatic cancellation threshold from 400 million to 800 million allowances from 1 January 2031**. Trilogue negotiations with the European Parliament now begin, with the aim of concluding this file by end-2026. This sits alongside the broader 17 July post-2030 ETS review (LRF cut, Industrial Decarbonisation Bank, ≥50% revenue earmarking), on which political agreement is still targeted for Q1 2027.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No direct bearing on the renewable-electricity instruments. Relevant only as an EU carbon-price signal affecting the value chain and Spanish operations feeding Scope 3; a higher effective cap/price tightening marginally raises the cost pressure behind supplier decarbonisation.
- **Link:** https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/09/23/market-stability-reserve-council-agrees-targeted-amendment-for-a-resilient-and-predictable-carbon-market/

## GHG Protocol — consolidated (ISO) corporate standard: no new material change

- **Source/date:** GHG Protocol — Consolidated Corporate Standard Development Plan (29 July 2026), confirmed in subsequent FAQ.
- **What changed:** No new substantive step since the 28 August digest. Subsequent commentary confirms the consolidated, ISO co-branded corporate standard (merging the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance, Scope 3 Standard, Actions & Market Instruments outputs and ISO 14064-1) is targeting an **integrated public consultation in Q2 2027 and final publication around Q4 2028**. The Scope 2 proposals (hourly/time-matched accounting, a deliverability test, legacy clause) and Scope 3 proposals (≥95% coverage, tiered data, Category 16) carry forward unchanged into this single process.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** The deliverability/hourly-matching and legacy-clause questions that govern long-run vPPA/REC/REV claimability under the market-based method remain on the single Q2 2027 consultation timeline — more runway to influence, no action required this week.
- **Link:** https://ghgprotocol.org/blog/ghg-protocol-announces-key-standard-development-updates

## CBAM — certificate sale/repurchase rules: operational detail only

- **Source/date:** European Commission — draft delegated regulation on CBAM certificate sale and repurchase; feedback period closed **6 August 2026**.
- **What changed:** The consultation on the certificate sale/repurchase delegated regulation (opened 9 July) closed 6 August. The rules confirm sales of CBAM certificates via a central platform will **start from 1 February 2027** (for 2026 imports), repurchase requests are due by 31 October each year, and the annual declaration/surrender deadline is 30 September of the following year. Operational implementation, not new scope.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** No bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments; watch only for input-cost effects on steel/aluminium/packaging feeding Scope 3.
- **Link:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en

## Fit for 55 / EU 2040 framework — no new material change

- **Source/date:** European Commission — 2040 Climate Law amendment (published in Official Journal 18 March 2026).
- **What changed:** No new development this period. The binding 90%-by-2040 net-reduction target is in force; delivery runs largely through the 17 July ETS review and the MSR file above. A dedicated post-2030 "Fit for 55"-style delivery package has not yet been tabled.
- **Relevance to Haleon:** Background context; no direct effect on the PPAs, REV instrument or the Scope 3 target.
- **Link:** https://commission.europa.eu/topics/climate-action/delivering-european-green-deal/fit-55-delivering-proposals_en
