# GHG Protocol — Actions and Market Instruments (AMI) (snapshot 2026-10-23)

- 60-day Request for Information ran 31 Mar–31 May 2026 on a proposed multi-statement reporting structure (physical inventory + market-based inventory + GHG impact statement).
- **No material change since 16 Oct.** AMI workstream remains consolidated with the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance and Scope 3 Standard into a single **Consolidated Corporate Standard** developed with ISO (integrating ISO 14064-1); Part 2 of the planned two-part standard covers market instruments. Standard Development Plan published 29 July 2026. Consolidated public-consultation draft expected **Q2 2027**; final ~2028. No interim AMI draft published.

- Standard Development Plan (29 Jul 2026): https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Consolidated-StandardDevelopmentPlan(SDP)-2026.07.29.pdf
