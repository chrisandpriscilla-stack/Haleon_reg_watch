# EU ETS — Emissions Trading System (snapshot 2026-10-23)

- Post-2030 review proposal (Phases 5 & 6; file **2026/0212 (COD)**) tabled by the Commission **17 July 2026**, aligning the system with the 2040 climate target.
- **No material change since 16 Oct.** First **Environment Council policy debate held 12 October 2026** (cap ambition, international credits and permanent removals, auction-revenue use, free allocation / carbon-leakage, scope extensions for aviation, maritime, waste). The Presidency is still aiming for a Council **general approach by December 2026** — earlier than the Q1 2027 political-agreement timeline previously flagged. No new Council/Parliament step this week; preparatory bodies continue work.
- Proposal substance unchanged: Linear Reduction Factor cut to **3.7% (2031–2035)** and **1.7% (2036+)** from 4.3%; new Article 9b for ~260m high-quality international credits; **€100bn Industrial Decarbonisation Bank** (Phase 1 "Investment Booster" 2028); Member States to earmark **≥50%** of auction revenue for decarbonisation; Market Stability Reserve intake rate cut **24%→12%**; municipal-waste incineration brought into scope from 2031; ~250Mt domestic permanent removals under discussion. ETS2 (buildings/road transport) confirmed for 2028. CBAM-linked free-allocation phase-out 2026–2036 continues.
- Analysts (Carbon Pulse) read the package as "materially bearish" for long-term prices. EUAs traded ~€82–84/t through recent months.
