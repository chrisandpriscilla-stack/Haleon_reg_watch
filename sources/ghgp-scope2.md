# GHG Protocol — Scope 2 Guidance revision (snapshot 2026-10-23)

- First public consultation ran Oct 2025–31 Jan 2026 (1,000+ submissions). Proposals: more time-specific (hourly) emission factors for both methods, tighter geographic/deliverability boundaries for market-based instruments, retained dual reporting (location- + market-based), legacy clause for existing contracts.
- **No material change since 16 Oct.** The Scope 2 Guidance remains folded into a single **Consolidated Corporate Standard** (prospectively Version 3.0) developed with ISO (per the 29 July 2026 Standard Development Plan), two-part structure: Part 1 general requirements + physical inventory, Part 2 market instruments. Consolidated draft for public consultation expected **Q2 2027**; final standard ~2028. No interim Scope 2 draft has been published.

- Consolidated Standard Development Plan (29 Jul 2026): https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/Consolidated-StandardDevelopmentPlan(SDP)-2026.07.29.pdf
