# Fit for 55 / EU 2040 climate framework (snapshot 2026-10-23)

- Fit for 55 implementation continues (≥55% by 2030). No new package.
- **No material change since 16 Oct.** The **90% net GHG reduction by 2040** target (Climate Law amendment) remains in force (effective April 2026), including limited use (~5%) of high-quality international credits and a route for permanent domestic carbon removals via the ETS.
- Delivery of the 2040 target runs primarily through the post-2030 EU ETS review (see eu-ets.md), now at Council (12 Oct 2026 policy debate, agreement targeted December 2026). No new delegated acts bearing on Haleon surfaced this period.
- Relevance to Haleon: background context; no direct effect on the PPAs, REV instrument or the Scope 3 target.
