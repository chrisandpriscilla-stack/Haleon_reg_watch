# Fit for 55 / EU 2040 climate framework (snapshot 2026-08-28)

- Fit for 55 implementation continues (≥55% by 2030). No new package.
- Since the 5 June baseline: the **90% net GHG reduction by 2040** target — an amendment to the European Climate Law — entered into force April 2026 (provisional political agreement Dec 2025), including limited use (up to ~5%) of high-quality international credits and a route for permanent domestic carbon removals via the ETS.
- GHG-removals integration consultation findings due ~July 2026; the 17 July 2026 EU ETS review (see eu-ets.md) is the main delivery lever for the 2040 target.
- Relevance to Haleon: background context; no direct effect on the PPAs, REV instrument or the Scope 3 target.
- Canonical page: https://commission.europa.eu/topics/climate-action/delivering-european-green-deal/fit-55-delivering-proposals_en
