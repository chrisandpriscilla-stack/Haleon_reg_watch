# CBAM — Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (snapshot 2026-10-23)

- Definitive regime live since 1 Jan 2026 (importers buy and surrender certificates for embedded emissions).
- **Scope-expansion legislative process — trilogue underway (no outcome yet):** the **first political trilogue on the CBAM scope-extension regulation was held 20 October 2026** as scheduled. No provisional agreement reached; a second (possibly final) round is provisionally set for **30 November 2026**, with institutions still targeting a deal before end-2026 for application from 1 January 2028. Positions going in: Commission ~180 downstream products, Council ~200 (general approach 12 June 2026), Parliament ~457 (first-reading mandate adopted 15 Sep 2026, backing 400+ downstream steel/aluminium products from 2028 and dropping the Commission price-shock "safety valve"); companion Temporary Decarbonisation Fund report also passed. Open points remain the product-list length, the crisis/safety-valve clause and anti-circumvention rules.
- **Certificate price:** Q2 2026 price €75.28 (published 6 July); **Q3 2026 price €82.32 (published 5 October 2026, +9.35% q/q)**. Next quarterly price due 4 January 2027. Routine operational data.
- **Certificate-trading rules:** consultation on the draft delegated regulation governing sale/repurchase of CBAM certificates (opened 9 July 2026) closed 6 August 2026; draft makes the CBAM registry the main channel and sets a €0.05/certificate fee. Not yet adopted.
- Earlier operational detail unchanged: 14 Aug 2026 the Commission published 10 guidance documents (4 general + 6 sector).
- Relevance to Haleon: no bearing on the vPPA/REC/REV electricity instruments; watch only for input-cost effects on steel/aluminium/packaging feeding Scope 3 — a wider downstream list from 2028 would broaden that exposure, but nothing binds until a final trilogue text is agreed.
