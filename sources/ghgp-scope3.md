# GHG Protocol — Scope 3 Standard revision (snapshot 2026-10-23)

- Phase 1 Progress Update published 31 Mar 2026 (first substantive output; 65-member Technical Working Group). Proposals: ≥95% coverage of required Scope 3 (≤5% exclusions), mandatory disaggregation by data tier, new Category 16 for other value-chain activities. Draft material, subject to change.
- **No material change since 16 Oct.** The Scope 3 Standard revision remains consolidated with the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance and Actions & Market Instruments into a single harmonised, ISO-partnered standard (prospectively Version 3.0). Consolidated public-consultation draft expected **Q2 2027**; final ~2028. No interim Scope 3 draft published.
