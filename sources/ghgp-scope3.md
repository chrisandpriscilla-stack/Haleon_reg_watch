# GHG Protocol — Scope 3 Standard revision (snapshot 2026-08-28)

- Phase 1 Progress Update published 31 Mar 2026 (first substantive output; 65-member Technical Working Group). Proposals: ≥95% coverage of required Scope 3 (≤5% exclusions), mandatory disaggregation by data tier, new Category 16 for other value-chain activities. Draft material, subject to change.
- **Material change (July 2026):** Scope 3 Standard revision now consolidated with the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance and Actions & Market Instruments into a single harmonised corporate GHG accounting standard, developed in partnership with ISO. Consolidated public-consultation draft expected **Q2 2027** (previously a standalone Scope 3 consultation was expected mid-2026 with final late 2027).
