# SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 (snapshot 2026-08-28)

- **Material change:** CNZS **Version 2.0 published 11 June 2026** — the most substantial revision to corporate target-setting since SBTi's founding (baseline of 5 June reported "not yet published").
- Implementation guidance and progress-assessment resources being released through 2026 and beyond; stakeholder feedback on draft implementation resources closed **31 July 2026**; final priority resources expected **Q4 2026** (before validations open).
- Companies can submit targets against V2 for validation from **early 2027**. V1.3.1 remains active for new targets in the interim (April 2026 V1.3.1 issued).
- Existing sector guidance (aviation, shipping, cement, steel, chemicals, buildings, FLAG) remains valid; sector resources to be updated for V2 compatibility, insight-gathering from key sectors began mid-2026.
