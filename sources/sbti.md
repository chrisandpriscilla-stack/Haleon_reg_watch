# SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 (snapshot 2026-10-16)

- CNZS **Version 2.0 published 11 June 2026** — the most substantial revision to corporate target-setting since SBTi's founding.
- **No material change since 28 Aug.** Implementation and progress-assessment resources being released through 2026 and beyond; stakeholder feedback on draft implementation resources closed 31 July 2026; final priority resources still expected **Q4 2026** (Renewal Policy expected this quarter; Ongoing Emissions Responsibility framework launches 2027). Companies can submit targets against V2 for validation from **1 February 2027**. V1.3.1 remains active for new targets in the interim.
- V2 structure: merges Near-Term Criteria into the Standard, ~15 implementation criteria with an action-prioritisation hierarchy; Scope 2 targets to be set on a location-based (physical) inventory; Scope 3 materiality threshold retained at 5%. Existing validated targets remain valid through their cycle, subject to 5-year review.
- Existing sector guidance (aviation, shipping, cement, steel, chemicals, buildings, FLAG) remains valid; sector approaches to be published by end-2027 and updated for V2 compatibility (automotive, power finalising; FLAG updating).
- Q4 2026 window now open — watch sciencebasedtargets.org for the Renewal Policy and V2 implementation resources landing.
