# SBTi — Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2 (snapshot 2026-10-23)

- CNZS **Version 2.0 published 11 June 2026** — the most substantial revision to corporate target-setting since SBTi's founding.
- **No material change since 16 Oct.** Implementation and progress-assessment resources being released through 2026 and beyond; stakeholder feedback on draft implementation resources closed 31 July 2026; final priority resources still expected **Q4 2026** (Renewal Policy expected this quarter; Ongoing Emissions Responsibility framework launches 2027). No new implementation resource or Renewal Policy publication surfaced this week. Companies can submit targets against V2 for validation from **1 February 2027**. V1.3.1 remains active for new targets in the interim (some commentators cite a later 31 Dec 2027 cut-off / Jan 2028 mandatory date — confirm against SBTi's own transition guidance when the final resources land).
- V2 structure: merges Near-Term Criteria into the Standard, ~15 implementation criteria with an action-prioritisation hierarchy (direct reductions, low-carbon electricity, projects, market instruments); Scope 2 targets to be set on a location-based (physical) inventory; Scope 3 materiality threshold retained at 5%. New assurance model. Existing validated targets remain valid through their cycle, subject to 5-year review.
- Existing sector guidance (aviation, shipping, cement, steel, chemicals, buildings, FLAG) remains valid; sector approaches to be published by end-2027 and updated for V2 compatibility.
