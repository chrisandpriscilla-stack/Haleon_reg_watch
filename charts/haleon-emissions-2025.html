Notes and sources

  1. Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3 figures are as reported in Haleon's 2025 ESG Databook (issued March 2026), pages 15–16. Scope 1 and 2 cover 1 December 2024 to 30 November 2025; Scope 3 covers 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025. 2024 Scope 3 figures were restated by Haleon for methodology and emission-factor changes.
  2. The five Scope 3 buckets group the databook's GHG Protocol categories and reconcile exactly to the reported 2,463 ktCO2e total. Purchased goods and services is category 1. Logistics is upstream plus downstream transportation and distribution (253 + 10). Other procurement managed is capital goods, fuel- and energy-related activities, waste generated in operations, business travel, employee commuting, upstream leased assets and investments (26 + 44 + 1 + 28 + 37 + 15 + 15). Use of goods sold is category 11 and end of life is category 12.
  3. Haleon reports Scope 2 of 125 ktCO2e location-based and 6 ktCO2e market-based, reflecting 100% renewable electricity. Net Scope 1 and 2 on a market-based basis is 43 ktCO2e after 20 ktCO2e of offsets — a 55% reduction against 2020.
  4. The 2%/5%/93% scope split is corroborated by Haleon's Climate Action Transition Plan (2025), page 7, which reports the same footprint from the same inventory.
